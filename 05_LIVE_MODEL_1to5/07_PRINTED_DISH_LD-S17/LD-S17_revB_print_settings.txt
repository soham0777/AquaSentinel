AFTER PRINTING - DO NOT SKIP
  1. Leak test FIRST: fill to 10 mm below the rim, stand it on dry paper towel for at least 1 hour
     (overnight is better). The electronics bay is directly under the dish - never put an untested
     dish on the model.
  2. If the towel is dry: ready to use. Nothing else needed.
  3. If it weeps anywhere: dry it fully, brush one thin coat of clear 2-part epoxy (or clear nail varnish)
     on the INSIDE over the wet spot and the floor corner, let it cure, repeat the leak test.
  4. Slide it onto the seat EMPTY, then fill.
  5. Water: pour about 400 mL (glass dish was 380 mL, rev A was 450 mL). The check is unchanged:
     water 1-2 mm over the cage plate, about 10 mm below the rim.
  6. No hot water and do not leave it in a hot car or in direct sun: PLA softens at about 55-60 C.

NOT YET VERIFIED
  The printed part has not been leak-tested. Watertightness depends on the print (dry filament,
  no under-extrusion), so step 1 above is the proof, not this file.
