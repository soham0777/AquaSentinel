Thursday 19 November · the booth

Dassault Systèmes Campus, Hinjewadi, Pune. The organisers ask every team to be in Pune by 10:00 and at the hotel by about 12:00. The exhibition fills the rest of the day; their travel guide has the detail.

Setting up, in this order

  • Table, power strip, model on its plinth.
  • Power the board first. The MQ-3 starts warming.
  • Fill the dish with clean tap water. Lower the cage.
  • pH cap off. Check the bulb is wet.
  • Pi on. Wait for the dashboard, then for green.
  • One full run of the six waters before anyone arrives.
  • Labels on the parts. Manuals and drawings laid out.
  • Real-or-simulated card standing where a visitor sees it.
  • Phone with the bench video and the SMS, charged.
