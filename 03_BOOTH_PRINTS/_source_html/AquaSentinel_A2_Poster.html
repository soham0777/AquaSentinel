AquaSentinel

At two in the morning a spill looks like water. Nobody is there to take the sample. So we built a float that is.

It rides the river

A float on a pole, clamped to a bridge pier. Eight rollers. When the river rises 2 m, so does the float. No motor. No anchor.

It reads the water

Every 15 minutes: pH, dissolved solids, turbidity, temperature. It learns what is normal there, and it notices when that changes.

It keeps the evidence

The light turns red. An SMS goes out. And a pump fills a bottle with that water, at that minute, for the lab.

What is on the table

A 1:5 model, printed in 37 parts. The sensors on it are not models. They are the real, full-size ones, seven of them, sitting in a dish of water.

Under the dish is a circuit board we drew and milled ourselves, and a Raspberry Pi that decides.

It names the kind of change. It does not name the chemical. That is what the bottle is for.
