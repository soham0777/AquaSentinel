Will the pole bend? Will the bracket hold?

Two static studies in SOLIDWORKS Simulation, run on the full-size CAD on 5 October 2026. Both parts are 316L stainless, yield 170 MPa. These are design-stage estimates. Nothing here has been load-tested.

Why our slides say 2.66 and this sheet says 4.4

The study fixes the pole rigidly at both ends, and real bolted clamps are not rigid. So we also did the sum by hand with the ends pinned and the load from the carriage added: 64.0 MPa, which is 170 ÷ 64.0 = 2.66. We quote 2.66 because it is the worse of the two.
