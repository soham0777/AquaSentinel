# Synthetic River Water-Quality Dataset — Godavari River Basin Pilot

> **TRANSPARENCY NOTICE (SCIENTIFIC HONESTY):**  
> This dataset (`synthetic_river_timeseries.csv`) consists of **synthetic lotic time-series data generated by numerical simulation** (`generate_water_quality_timeseries.py`). It is **NOT measured field data**. In strict adherence to our engineering honesty rules, it must be cited everywhere as synthetic benchmark data used for algorithm training and test harness verification pending permitted river deployment.

---

## 1. Simulation Parameters & Physical Models

The data generator simulates 30 continuous days of 15-minute sampling (2,880 rows) calibrated to typical lotic river conditions in the upper Godavari River basin near Kopargaon, Maharashtra:

* **Diurnal Photosynthetic & Thermal Cycling:**
  - Water Temperature: Sinusoidal daily fluctuation between $21.5^\circ\text{C}$ (dawn) and $27.0^\circ\text{C}$ (afternoon peak).
  - Dissolved Oxygen (DO): Photosynthetic daytime supersaturation by benthic algae ($7.5–8.2\text{ mg/L}$) and nighttime respiration minimum ($5.8–6.4\text{ mg/L}$).
  - pH: Carbonate-bicarbonate equilibrium driven by diurnal $CO_2$ drawdown ($7.3–7.8$).
  - Electrical Conductivity (EC): Baseline lotic background ($320–420\ \mu\text{S/cm}$).
  - Turbidity: Normal dry-season baseflow ($6–14\text{ NTU}$).
  - River Stage: Baseflow elevation with seasonal stage fluctuations ($1.2–1.8\text{ m}$).

## 2. Simulated Contamination Events (Test Harness Triggers)

To validate the onboard anomaly detection and robotic autosampler trigger, 4 episodic pollution incidents are injected into the time-series:

1. **Event 1 (Day 5, 02:00 UTC) — Midnight Industrial Acid Effluent Dump:**
   - Abrupt pH collapse to $4.8$ (violating CPCB Class C minimum 6.0).
   - Surge in Electrical Conductivity to $890\ \mu\text{S/cm}$.
   - Duration: 3.5 hours.
2. **Event 2 (Day 12, 14:00 UTC) — Untreated Municipal Sewage Shock Load:**
   - Severe hypoxia: Dissolved Oxygen drops to $2.1\text{ mg/L}$ (violating CPCB minimum 4.0 mg/L).
   - Electrical Conductivity spikes to $1,420\ \mu\text{S/cm}$.
   - Turbidity rises to $65\text{ NTU}$.
   - Duration: 6 hours.
3. **Event 3 (Day 19, 09:00 UTC) — Monsoon Agricultural Runoff & Siltation:**
   - Extreme turbidity spike to $450\text{ NTU}$ (heavy suspended solids).
   - Moderate EC rise ($680\ \mu\text{S/cm}$).
   - River stage rise of $+0.85\text{ m}$.
   - Duration: 12 hours.
4. **Event 4 (Day 26, 22:00 UTC) — Alkaline Chemical Distillery Discharge:**
   - Abrupt pH surge to $9.6$ (violating CPCB Class C maximum 9.0).
   - Electrical Conductivity exceeds $2,450\ \mu\text{S/cm}$ (violating CPCB Class C limit 2250 µS/cm).
   - Duration: 4 hours.

## 3. Dataset Schema (2,880 Rows, 10 Columns)

| Column Header | Unit | Physical Range | Measurement Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `timestamp_utc` | ISO 8601 | 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-31 | Timestamped 15-min cadence |
| `epoch_time` | Seconds | Unix Epoch Timestamp | Monotonic integer |
| `ph` | pH units | 4.5 – 10.0 | Simulated RS485 probe (SEN0708) |
| `ec_us_cm` | µS/cm | 250 – 3,000 | Simulated RS485 probe (SEN0707) |
| `do_mg_l` | mg/L | 1.5 – 10.0 | Simulated Optical DO (SEN0680) |
| `turbidity_ntu` | NTU | 2 – 600 | Simulated Nephelometric (SEN0710) |
| `temp_c` | °C | 18.0 – 32.0 | Probe integrated PT1000 |
| `stage_m` | Meters | 0.5 – 3.0 | Ultrasonic stage sensor (JSN-SR04T) |
| `battery_v` | Volts | 12.6 – 13.8 | Simulated LiFePO4 battery bus |
| `true_event_label`| String | `BASELINE`, `ACID_DUMP`, etc. | Ground truth benchmark label |
