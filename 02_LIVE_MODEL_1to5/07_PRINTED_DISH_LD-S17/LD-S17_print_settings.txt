FIT IN THE MODEL (same outside size as the glass dish)
  OD 115 mm  -> seat LD-S09 stop bore is 116.5 mm: 0.75 mm play per side, slides in from the front
  Height 65  -> rim stays 5 mm under the float hulls; parked cage still 6 mm above the rim
  Wall 0.5   -> inside 114 mm: cage (dia 92) is now 11 mm from the wall (glass: 8.5 mm)
  Floor 1.0  -> 5 layers of 0.2 mm; 0.6 mm chamfer on the outer bottom edge (elephant-foot relief)

WHY TWO FILAMENTS (the 20 g limit)
  Whole dish in one material  : about 27 g (wall 14.3 g + floor 12.9 g)
  Thinnest possible, one material (0.45 wall, 0.6 floor): about 20.7 g  -> over 20 g, and the floor would leak
  SOLUTION: floor in ANY other PLA (~13 g, hidden in the seat), wall in TRANSPARENT PLA
  Transparent used: ~13-14 g wall + ~1 g purge at the swap = ~15 g  (about 5 g margin)

SLICER SETTINGS (0.4 mm nozzle)
  Layer height ............ 0.2 mm (first layer 0.2 mm)
  Spiral / vase mode ...... ON   (Cura: "Spiralize Outer Contour"; Prusa/Orca/Bambu: "Spiral vase")
  Bottom layers ........... 5    (= 1.0 mm floor); top layers 0; infill 0 %
  Wall / outer line width . 0.50 mm
  Filament change ......... at the start of layer 6 (Z = 1.2 mm): swap to TRANSPARENT PLA
      Bambu / Orca / Prusa: right-click the layer slider at 1.2 mm -> Add pause (or Add color change)
      Cura: Extensions > Post Processing > Modify G-Code > Filament Change (or Pause at height) > layer 6
  Nozzle temp ............. 220 C (clearer, better layer bonding = fewer leaks); bed 60 C
  Wall speed .............. 25-30 mm/s
  Part fan ................ 50 %
  Flow .................... 100-105 %
  Skirt 1 loop (prints in the floor filament), no brim, no supports
  CHECK the slicer's filament estimate before printing: the transparent part (layer 6 up) must be under ~16 g

AFTER PRINTING
  1. Leak test FIRST: fill with water and leave it on paper towel for 1 hour. The electronics bay is
     directly under the dish - never put an untested dish on the model.
  2. If it weeps: coat the inside floor joint and the wall thinly with clear epoxy or clear nail varnish, dry, test again.
  3. Slide it onto the seat EMPTY, then fill. Do not lift it full of water by the rim (thin wall).
  4. Water: about 450 mL now (was 380 mL with glass). The check is unchanged:
     water 1-2 mm over the cage plate, about 10 mm below the rim.
  5. No hot water: PLA softens at about 55-60 C.

GLASS OPTION (if you still want glass)
  115 x 65 mm is a standard 500 mL laboratory CRYSTALLISING DISH (e.g. DURAN 213134901).
  Ask a lab-glassware / scientific supplies shop, not a kitchen shop.
