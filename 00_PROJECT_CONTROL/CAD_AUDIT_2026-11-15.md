# CAD audit — 15 November 2026

The request was to go through the 268 SOLIDWORKS files and keep only what
belongs to the two real models: the full-size **R3** (66 parts) and the **1:5
LIVE** model (37–40 printed parts), including the parts designed with **Vector**.
Everything unrelated or old was to be removed.

I opened every current assembly read-only in SOLIDWORKS, walked its component
tree, and checked that each referenced file exists on disk. The per-file
verdicts, sizes, SHA-256 hashes and reference evidence are in
`CAD_AUDIT_2026-11-15.csv` (212 rows).

### The 40-part trap

The first reference read showed the 1:5 assembly pointing at
`12_ELECTRONICS_MANUAL_1to5/CAD/live_copy/`. I removed the 40 local parts in
`05_SOLIDWORKS_ASSEMBLY/` as byte-identical duplicates, then re-opened the
assembly in a clean SOLIDWORKS session. It reported **40 missing references**
(`.../05_SOLIDWORKS_ASSEMBLY/LD-F05.SLDPRT` … `P-SEN0189.SLDPRT`).

The first read had been contaminated: the live_v2 assembly was already loaded,
and SOLIDWORKS reused those already-open documents because the filenames and
bytes were identical. The 1:5 assembly's **stored** references are to its own
local parts. They are not duplicates in function, so they must stay.

I restored all 40 from `assembly_parts_copy_2026-11-07.zip` and verified 40/40
SHA-256 hashes. The assembly now opens with **40/40 references and 0 missing**.
The zip remains as a spare copy; it is not required while the 40 parts are on
disk.

### E02 and E03

`E02_ADS1115_ADC` and `E03_DS3231_RTC` belong to the earlier design; the
final electronics has no ADS1115 and no RTC. But they are still **inserted in
live_v2 and bay_v2**. Deleting them would break the current CAD assemblies, so
they stay. If the v2 electronics assembly is ever cleaned up, they can go at
the same time as that assembly.

## 5. “Vector” was not junk

Vector is the AI engineering copilot with the SOLIDWORKS bridge that generated
the 1:5 model. The 17 named parts in `02_LIVE_MODEL_1to5/02_CAD_SOLIDWORKS/`
are its design sources (31 Oct 2026), and `LD-S16_sample_bottle_printable` is
the current 13 Nov variant. They are **kept**: they are the only descriptively
named, feature-tree source of the 1:5 parts.

The 40 parts in `05_SOLIDWORKS_ASSEMBLY/` are the working set the assembly
actually references. They are byte-identical to the 40 in
`12_ELECTRONICS_MANUAL_1to5/CAD/live_copy/`, but they are not interchangeable
at the file-reference level. Both sets stay.

## 6. What is kept, and why

- **R3, 67 files** — the complete full-size prototype; the assembly resolves
  66/66 parts. This is the “big model”.
- **1:5 working assembly, 41 files** — the complete printed model; 40/40 parts
  resolve. This is the “1.5 model”.
- **1:5 Vector design, 18 files** — the named AI-designed source parts.
- **1:5 electronics, 76 files** — E01–E19 and the sensor/module models used by
  live_v2 and bay_v2. This includes E02/E03, which are legacy electrically but
  still in the current CAD. It also includes `bay_v2`, the Hub-HAT-era bay
  layout; the final electronics is the Sensor Hub PCB v1.2, but the bay assembly
  is intact and shares parts with live_v2. It is not used in the final manual.
- **FEA, 2 files** — the current guide-pole and pier-bracket studies.
- **Printed dish, 1 file** — the Fusion 360 source of the Ø115 × 65 mm dish.

## 7. Can it go below 205?

Only by changing current work, not by deleting leftovers:

- **40-part merge** — repoint the 1:5 assembly to `live_copy` and save it, or
  vice versa. That would remove 40 files but makes one assembly depend on the
  other's part set; it needs a careful SOLIDWORKS “Replace References” pass.
- **17 Vector sources** — archive the 31 Oct named parts. That would remove 17
  files but lose the only named, AI-generated design source. Not recommended
  before the 19–20 Nov event.
- **bay_v2 layout** — archive the abandoned Hub-HAT bay. That would remove 1
  assembly, but it is an intact CAD view and its parts are still used by
  live_v2.

None of these makes the project more correct for the booth. If the goal is a
small “booth-only” copy, copy the deliverable folders; do not delete the source.
