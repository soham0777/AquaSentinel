## Print-shop instructions to hand over

1. Colour, 100% scale, no "fit to page" — the drawings and the check sheet must stay at A3.
2. A2 poster on 200 gsm, matte; sharpest text at the top, no gloss (glare under hall lights).
3. Bind the 1:5 manual with the addendum as page 2; bind the electronics manual with the PCB guide at the back.
4. Sticker sheets: matt, laser-safe; test one label before cutting the sheet.
5. Trim the A5 cards to 148 × 210 mm; laminate the two that go on the table.
