AquaSentinel 1:5 LIVE model: pre-flight checklist

Hardware truth day, Sunday 15 November 2026 · Team Sanjivani Hero's Journey · AG26-1500$41 · Do the sections in order. Write every number down: the deck is corrected from this sheet.

Exit criterion for today: one real run on real hardware: water changes → class on the dashboard → red beacon → pump → SMS on a phone. Not the simulator.

A. Put today's code on the Pi (5 min)

  • Copy the folder 14_RASPBERRY_PI_SOFTWARE/aquasentinel from the laptop to the Pi at ~/aquasentinel, replacing the old copy. The laptop copy was fixed on 15 Nov (MQ-3 warm-up guard).
  • On the Pi: cd ~/aquasentinel then python3 tools/self_test.py. It must print 7 lines and ALL PASS. If it prints 6 lines, the Pi still has the old copy.
  • Nano firmware: #define BOARD_PCB 1 in aquasentinel_nano.ino, then upload (Arduino Nano, ATmega328P Old Bootloader).

B. Power, with nothing plugged into J15 and J17 (10 min)

  • All power OFF while wiring. Never more than 5 V into J12.
  • AUX 5 V charger on J12. Measured at J12: V (expect 4.9 to 5.2).
  • Step-up on J14. Measured output: V (set to 9.0).
  • Step-down on J16. Measured output: V (set to 4.0, never above 4.2).
  • Power OFF. Now plug the pH board power into J15 and the SIM800L into J17. Antenna on, SIM in.

C. Wiring (10 min)

  • J1 MQ-3 (DO → D6); its AO to J10 pin A3
  • J2 turbidity (DO → D5); its AO to J10 pin A2
  • J3 LDR (DO → D4)
  • J4 DHT11 (D3)
  • J5 DS18B20 (D2)
  • J6 RGB beacon (G D7, R D8, B D9)
  • J7 pump control, J13 pump power to the Mini L298N
  • J9 TDS (power A0, signal A1)
  • J11 pH signal pin "O" (A6)
  • J18 → Pi: GND pin 6, TX pin 8, RX pin 10
  • Nano USB cable into the Pi. All probes in clean tap water. pH electrode cap off, bulb wet.

D. Hardware check (5 min)

  • sudo systemctl stop aquasentinel then python3 ~/aquasentinel/tools/check_hardware.py
  • "readings in 10 s" count: (expect about 10). Every sensor line says OK.
  • Beacon goes red, green, blue, green. Pump runs 2 s when you answer y (its end in water).
  • SIM800L answers · SIM card OK · signal /31 (10+ is good) · network registered.

Any FAIL or CHECK: fix it and run the check again before going on. Write here what failed:
