# Creative Experience and storytelling: everything in one place

Collected 13 Nov 2026. The earlier `10_VIRTUAL_STORYTELLER` folder was superseded by this one and deleted on 14 Nov 2026.
Use this folder — it is the only current one.

| Folder / file | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `05_CXP_renders/` | Render made **in 3DEXCITE Creative Experience** on the platform (R3 at sunset over water) | Booth screen, FDC hero image |
| On the 3DEXPERIENCE platform | Experience **"Creative Experience00011982"** in *soham k personal space*, with our R3 (`AquaSentinel_R3_MidWater`) and the ocean-sunset ambience | Open it on the booth laptop and orbit the float live |
| `04_story_video/AquaSentinel_Story_A_Night_on_the_Godavari_1080p.mp4` | 66 s story video, no music. Made by us from our CAD in a web renderer, **not** in Creative Experience | Booth screen loop; call it "3D animation of our CAD" |
| `02_CXP_2D_assets/` | 35 graphics: title card, act banners, SMS phone, buttons, labels | Overlays in Creative Experience or the deck |
| `03_AR_model/` | Cleaned R3 model (`.glb`) + a 3D viewer page | Only if you decide to publish it for a QR code |
| `01_Creative_Experience_Storyline_and_Build_Plan.md` | The 8-act story "A night on the Godavari" and the build steps | Next steps in Creative Experience |
| `00_Virtual_Storyteller_Plan.md` | First plan (7 Nov) | Background |
| `06_source_scripts/` | Scripts that made the graphics, the story video and the AR page | Rebuilding them |
| `07_PCB_picture_guides/` | Picture cards for soldering, beep tests, power-up, plugging in each sensor, and the tray layout | Assembling the Sensor Hub PCB in the 1:5 model |

How to open Creative Experience: platform compass -> Virtual Storyteller for Education -> Creative Experience (never start 3DEXPERIENCE.exe directly: that gives the "tenant OnPremise" error).
