## Discrepancies between visuals, drawings, CAD and software

* Renders, GIFs, the assembly video and the viewer all come from the same GLB as the STEP/STL exports, so geometry agrees.
* The KPI tile and pitch video quote the fixed-end FEA safety factor (4.4); the conservative pinned value is 3.1 (2.66 with the carriage couple).
* The dashboard and v2 documents show borosilicate vials and a 4 °C cooler; R3 CAD has HDPE bottles and no cooling.
* DWG-05 marks handle inserts on one side of a hull made in a single mould; the CAD mounts handles outboard on both hulls.
* The anatomy image labels the stage sensor as reading the river level; the stage firmware is a constant.
* The 'weathervane' render shows the float in the pier's lee, where the uniform-flow weathervane argument does not strictly hold.
