AquaSentinel

Watches the river. Warns the bank.
Keeps the evidence.

A solar-powered float on a guide pole, clamped to a bridge pier. Every 15 minutes it reads the river; when a spill passes it turns its beacon red, sends an alert and seals a time-stamped water sample. The river moves it, not a motor.

How it works

Sense. Decide. Act.

🔬

Sense

Every 15 minutes, on the float itself.

  • pH, conductivity, optical DO, turbidity
  • temperature and river level
  • probes on a pull-up cartridge with a wiper
🧠

Decide

On the ESP32, no cloud needed.

  • CPCB hard limits act immediately
  • site baseline learned in the first 7 days
  • acts when 2 of the last 3 readings are unusual
🚨

Act

Warn, alert, keep the proof.

  • 360° beacon turns red for the bank
  • SMS / LTE alert to the water-works
  • 250 mL sample sealed and time-stamped
  • A float on a pole, not a boat. A 2 in SS316L pole is clamped to the bridge pier; an 8-roller carriage lets the float ride 2 m of river level.
  • Weathervane by design. The pole sits at the bow, so the current turns the float and the pole carries no torque.
  • Zero watts to hold station. A 20 W solar canopy shades the pod and charges a LiFePO4 battery.
  • Off before a flood, no tools. The front of the carriage is a gate held by two T-handle pins.

Inside

Decides on the float.
No cloud needed.

Hard limits act immediately: anything outside CPCB Class C is an alert. For the first 7 days the float learns what normal looks like at its site, hour by hour, then acts when 2 of the last 3 readings are unusual, so a single splash cannot trigger a sample.

The design journey

Our own CAD caught our biggest mistake.

In v2 the guide pole ran straight through the electronics box. Moving it to the bow fixed the clash and turned it into a feature: the float now weathervanes with the current and the pole carries no torque.
