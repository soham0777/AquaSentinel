AquaSentinel

A river that raises its own alarm.

A floating sentinel on a bridge pier. Every 15 minutes it reads the water; the moment a spill passes, it turns its beacon red, sends an SMS and bottles a sample for the lab.

The hidden crisis

2:00 a.m. A spill passes.
By sunrise, the river looks clean.

Tankers pull up to the bank, a hose goes into the river, and they are gone before sunrise. Families downstream drink from the town intake; farmers irrigate with the same water. Nobody saw it, because rivers are checked about once a month, and spills last hours.

Our innovation

A sentinel that lives in the river
and decides on the spot.

01
📡

Sense

Every 15 minutes: pH, dissolved solids, turbidity and water temperature; rain, light and humidity; and vapour from the sample bottle. 2,880 readings a month instead of one.

02
🧠

Decide

On board, no cloud. A Raspberry Pi compares each reading with hard limits and with what is normal for that hour of the day, and names the pattern: industrial, acidic, runoff or solvent.

03
🚨

Act

In seconds. A red beacon for anyone on the bank, an SMS to the water-works and the board, and one bottle of the spill for the lab, so the right water gets tested.

A simulator of our designed rules: pH below 6 or above 9 and dissolved solids above 1,400 ppm act at once; turbidity at 3× its hourly normal; the pattern names the class. The real system also needs 2 of 3 unusual readings for slow changes, and learns its baselines for 7 days.

Innovation 1 · It rides the river

No anchors.
No motor.
The river lifts it.

A two-inch pole is clamped to a bridge pier, and eight rollers let the float rise and fall two metres with the water. The current turns it like a weathervane, and for service the carriage gate simply opens.

The electronics

One Raspberry Pi. Eight sensors.
Three actions.

Thirteen boards modelled as native SOLIDWORKS parts, assembled on the printed tray, checked for clashes (two found, two fixed, now zero) and documented in drawing AS-LIVE-ELEC-01. Hover a pin on the header to see what it does.
