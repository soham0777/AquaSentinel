**What is new since the FDC**

- **A 1:5 live demonstrator of AquaSentinel R3, designed in SOLIDWORKS.**
  - The 1:5 float runs on its guide rod beside the pier column.
  - Real, full-size sensors (pH, TDS, turbidity, water temperature and a sampling pump) hang under the float in a 1:1 sensor cage and dip into a glass dish of water.
  - The Raspberry Pi electronics sit in the plinth.
  - A met head on the column carries the rain, light (LDR) and air temperature/humidity (DHT11) sensors.
  - An MQ-3 sniffs the vapour above a sample bottle, and a red/green LED beacon shows the alert state.
- **37 printed parts**, 17 of them new for the live model. Printing and assembly are in progress, and the electronics and software are still to be built. We plan to show the model at the booth on 19 November.
- **The full-size R3 design is unchanged:** the float, guide carriage, sensor cartridge, autosampler and solar canopy as submitted at the FDC.
